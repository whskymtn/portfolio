## Project Overview
Designed a low power pseudo-random LED blinker circuit. Taught wiring and soldering to team members. Fabricated new LED signs, and field-tested/repaired previous lights and signs. Managed lighting install and strike teams.

* **Circuit Design**: Custom low-power pseudo-random LED blinking circuit
* **Team Leadership**: Technical training in soldering and field wiring
* **Operations**: On-site management of installation and strike teams
